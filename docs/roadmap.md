# Verification milestones

These are outstanding proof tasks, not results inferred from a successful build.

1. **Global convergence:** formalize nonnegative-supported finite Laplace measures, the no-atom/extended-moment equivalence, one-line four-dimensional polar/Gamma integration including the massless endpoint, and lower-moment implications. Prove the selected-routing change of variables with its Fourier and determinant factors. Then prove the complete selected-line theorem and the original global Gaussian/Schwinger theorem with its infrared envelopes and exact endpoint order.
2. **O--QED measure:** prove the stated stable-half subordination and probability normalization; finiteness of every inverse moment; the Bessel/Gamma values and the three displayed integer moments; positive exponential moments including the endpoint. Connect these to the routed graph theorem without adding a positive photon mass.
3. **Obstructions:** formalize the soft Ward diagonal cancellation, Clifford traces, nonzero-transfer asymptotics, and the raw constant-background contact flux. Prove the actual nonintegrability statements. An algebraic cancellation or a numerical quadrature is not a substitute for these claims.
4. **Analyticity and scalar limits:** build the local common-majorant holomorphy theorem, complex tube, derivative budgets, stable-half nonanalyticity, Gaussian remainder, depth crossovers, and the common scalar subtraction before taking zero depth.
5. **Carrier, source and BRST/BV:** identify the quotient carrier with the two-character model; model graded source derivatives and nilpotent BRST on an explicit algebra; establish the master-action and functional identities on a stated domain. Infinite-dimensional path-integral changes of variables require a rigorous construction or an explicitly formal perturbative interpretation.
6. **Physical and response interfaces:** formalize the heat-damping positivity counterexample, physical spectral obstruction, finite response error/Schur and pole estimates. Physical reconstruction and cut matching remain assumptions until independently constructed. The crossing step requires the precise hypotheses of its external theorems.

The graph convergence and symmetry milestones must concern the **same specified completed action** before they can be combined. Graphwise results provide no convergence theorem for the infinite perturbation series.

Each contribution should close a precise source-to-Lean gap in [claims.md](claims.md). Keep failed criteria distinct from proofs of divergence, and comparison models distinct from a completed physical theory.
