# Remaining verification work

Full manuscript verification is still open. The complete inventory in [claims.md](claims.md) records every named source result; a successful build does not close entries marked partial or pending.

1. **Routed graph convergence.** Exact four-dimensional line moments, lower-moment transfer and independent scale/product integration are checked. The selected affine routing/Jacobian/Fourier theorem, real-degree routed numerator bounds and general Schwinger-envelope graph bounds are now checked. Complete rectangular rank-to-selected-row construction and the exact original global-statement assembly. The Gaussian determinant/weighted-shift proof can be verified separately as an alternative derivation. Concrete sector applications must prove their covering/Jacobian obligations.
2. **The kernel's exact transform.** The literal density's inverse moments and endpoint exponential tilt are checked. General-tempering normalization, genuine square-root subordination, dimensionful heat scaling and the complete exponential-moment threshold/formulas are now proved. Complete the Bessel/general-tempering inverse-moment formula and the three displayed polynomial specializations. Do not infer the transform identity from moment finiteness.
3. **Action-generated obstructions.** The displayed Ward and combined contact traces are genuinely nonintegrable. Derive those traces from explicit Clifford matrices and Ward/contact vertices, including their signs and multiplicities. Complete exact radial flux/exhaustion identities, nonzero-transfer angular tests, the general radial-damper obstruction and the smooth-probe/spatial-wave-packet form of the heat-damping OS counterexample; the literal complex Fourier/Hankel obstruction is proved.
4. **Analyticity and scalar limits.** Actual positive-depth scalar integrals and depth differentiation have been formalized. Finish the sharp derivative constants and auxiliary-mass uniformization, complex-depth/external-momentum holomorphy, Cauchy budgets and tubes, stable-half nonanalyticity, Gaussian entire remainder, strong-depth crossovers, common subtracted zero-depth logarithmic limit/slope and unsubtracted finite part.
5. **Source and BRST/BV framework.** The scalar carrier quotient/reality and character estimates are complete. Define a concrete graded field/source/antifield function algebra, its derivative conventions, completed master action and an invariant integration or formal Wick expectation on a common domain. Prove nilpotence and the conditional change-of-variables/Legendre derivation, then background Ward/Nielsen identities. The same action's graphs and insertions must meet the analytic estimates. The unbounded free-source spectral core and finite-mode Jacobian arguments are separate remaining constructions.
6. **Physical and pole interfaces.** Bounded-operator Neumann, Schur, analytic inverse and contour error estimates are checked. Finish the graph-domain version, analytic root-family/residue identification, root counts and shifted-pole remainder. Formalize the physical spectral obstruction and conditional external reconstruction/crossing theorems with their exact mass-gap, locality, LSZ and boundary hypotheses.

The paper's OS-completion and cut-matching assumptions remain assumptions. Proving a conditional theorem about a rigorously defined structure does not require proving every physical premise; it does require defining that structure and deriving its conclusion from the stated premises. Adding the desired conclusion as a project-specific axiom would not accomplish that.

Graphwise convergence at fixed positive depth, a completed interaction, convergence of the infinite perturbation series, and physical scattering are distinct obligations. The repository does not silently promote one into another.
